CivCentral — 5 logo concepts

Each shown as an app icon + horizontal lockup, on light and dark. SVGs live next to this file.

1 · Voxel Tile — pixel “C” app mark

Most Minecraft-native. A pixel-art “C” on a rounded emerald tile. Reads instantly as a game/community app icon, scales down to a favicon cleanly, and is the easiest to animate. Risk: leans playful, less “archive”.

2 · Compass Hub — “all roads meet here”

Best “central / hub” story. Spokes converging on a golden core — a meeting point of civilizations. Single-weight, works as a tiny monochrome glyph, very legible. Risk: generic compass feel unless paired with the wordmark.

3 · Iso Ziggurat — a built civilization

Most “civilization-building”. A stepped isometric tower with a banner on top — evokes Minecraft builds and rising empires. Strong, distinctive silhouette. Risk: the most detailed mark; loses fidelity at favicon size.

4 · Civic Crest — heraldic archive

Most “lore / heritage”. A shield + castle crest — gravitas that suits an authoritative archive and looks great on a Discord server icon. Risk: crests are common in gaming; relies on the gold/emerald palette to feel ownable.

5 · Hex Cube — modern block emblem

Most scalable / brandable. An isometric block reading as a hexagon badge — “a block” (Minecraft) and “a hub facet” at once. Minimal, flat, perfect at every size, ages well. Risk: cleanest but least overtly themed. My pick for a primary mark.
